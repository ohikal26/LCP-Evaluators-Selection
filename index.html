Your LCP evaluators

The people who will give you feedback on the Leadership Circle Profile.

Add everyone you want to hear from, then press Download the list at the bottom and send the file back to your coach. That is all you need to do here.

Each of them gets a short online questionnaire. Their answers are anonymous. You see the results grouped by relationship, never by name, and a group is only shown separately when enough people in it respond. That is why the numbers below matter: if you name only two peers, you lose the peer view altogether.

Pick people who see you work and will tell you the truth, not only the ones who like you. Nothing is sent from this page. It stays in your browser until you download the file.
